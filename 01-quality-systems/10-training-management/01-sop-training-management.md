# 01-SOP-TRN: Training Management

> **Use this SOP to:** assign, complete, assess, restrict, and record training for personnel performing GxP work.

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the training system for GMP roles and activities.

It ensures personnel have the education, training, experience, and continuing familiarity needed to perform assigned GxP functions.

## 2. Scope

Applies to employees, contractors, temporary staff, consultants, and supplier personnel performing or supporting GMP work, including:

- manufacturing, packaging, warehouse, laboratory, engineering, maintenance, calibration, cleaning, QA, validation, IT, and quality system activities
- supervisors and reviewers
- personnel using GMP computerized systems
- personnel creating, reviewing, or approving GMP records

## 3. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.25, 211.28, 211.34.
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 training expectations for electronic record/signature systems where applicable.
- FDA Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP guidance.
- EU GMP Chapter 1, Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- EU GMP Chapter 2, Personnel.
- EU GMP Chapter 4, Documentation.
- ICH Q10, Pharmaceutical Quality System.

## 4. Training Types

| Type | Use |
| --- | --- |
| GMP induction | Before independent GMP work. |
| Role-based SOP training | Before performing or approving a task. |
| Practical/on-the-job training | For hands-on operations, equipment, systems, cleaning, sampling, or lab methods. |
| Qualification / competency assessment | For critical or complex tasks. |
| Refresher training | Periodic reinforcement of GMP, GDP, data integrity, and role-specific topics. |
| Remedial training | After deviation, audit finding, observation, or performance gap. |
| Change training | Before using revised documents, systems, processes, or equipment. |

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Training Owner / Training Department | Maintains training system, matrices, assignments, records, and overdue reporting. |
| Department Manager | Defines role requirements, ensures personnel complete training before independent work, and confirms competency. |
| Trainer | Delivers training and assesses understanding or performance. |
| Trainee | Completes assigned training honestly and asks for clarification before performing work. |
| Quality Unit | Approves GMP training strategy, critical training content, and remediation approach where quality impact exists. |
| Document Owner | Defines training impact for new or revised documents. |

## 5.1 Training Scenario Rules

| Scenario | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| New employee or contractor starts GMP work | Assign induction, GDP/data integrity, role SOPs, safety/area training, and supervised work restrictions before independent work. |
| SOP, system, equipment, or process changes | Assign change training before effective use; block independent work if critical training is incomplete. |
| Critical task requires qualification | Use observed practical assessment or supervised runs; record pass/fail and authorization status. |
| Training is overdue | Restrict affected GMP task until training is complete or Quality approves interim supervision. |
| Deviation suggests knowledge gap | Define targeted remedial training linked to root cause; do not use generic retraining as default CAPA. |

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Define role requirements

Each GMP role must have a training profile or matrix defining:

- required GMP/GDP/data integrity induction
- required SOPs and procedures
- required systems and equipment
- practical qualification requirements
- refresher frequency
- supervisor/reviewer requirements

### 6.2 Assign training

Training must be assigned:

- at onboarding
- before role change or new task
- before effective use of new/revised SOPs, systems, equipment, or processes
- after relevant deviation, CAPA, audit finding, complaint, or regulatory observation
- at defined refresher intervals

### 6.3 Conduct training

Training may include:

- self-read with acknowledgement
- instructor-led session
- practical demonstration
- supervised execution
- computer-based training
- assessment or quiz
- observed competency check

For critical tasks, read-and-understand alone is usually not enough. Use practical demonstration or competency assessment.

### 6.4 Competency assessment

Competency should be assessed for tasks where incorrect performance could affect product quality, patient safety, data integrity, validated state, or regulatory commitments.

Assessment may include:

- direct observation
- practical test
- sample record review
- mock scenario
- system workflow execution
- calculation or method challenge
- supervisor sign-off after supervised runs

### 6.5 Training before independent work

Personnel must not independently perform GMP tasks until required training and qualification are complete or a documented supervised training plan is approved.

If urgent work is needed before full qualification, Quality and the Department Manager must approve controls such as direct supervision, second-person verification, or task restriction.

### 6.6 Remedial training

Remedial training after a deviation or error must be targeted to the root cause.

Do not use retraining as the only CAPA unless the investigation shows the issue was caused by a training or knowledge gap and the training action is designed to prevent recurrence.

### 6.7 Training effectiveness

Effectiveness may be checked through:

- observation
- quiz results
- record review
- reduction in related deviations
- supervisor review
- internal audit
- competency recheck

### 6.8 Training records

Training records must be GDP-compliant and include:

- trainee name
- training item/title/version
- trainer or system
- date completed
- assessment result where applicable
- signature/electronic acknowledgement
- qualification or restriction status

## 7. Overdue Training

Training overdue must be escalated based on risk.

If overdue training affects a person's ability to perform a GMP task, the Department Manager must restrict independent task performance until training is complete or a documented interim control is approved.

## 8. Contractors and Consultants

Contractors and consultants must be trained proportionate to their GMP impact.

At minimum, define:

- role and permitted activities
- required GMP/GDP/data integrity training
- site safety and contamination controls where applicable
- supervision requirements
- record and confidentiality expectations

Consultant qualifications must be retained where required.

## 9. Metrics

Track:

- training completion rate
- overdue training by department and criticality
- qualification status for critical tasks
- training-related deviations
- remedial training effectiveness
- refresher completion

Metrics should feed management review.

## 10. Quick Checklist

- Is the role training profile defined?
- Is training complete before independent work?
- Are critical tasks competency-assessed?
- Are revised documents linked to training impact?
- Are contractors trained and supervised appropriately?
- Are overdue training risks controlled?

*This SOP aligns with FDA personnel qualification and continuing CGMP training expectations, EU GMP personnel and PQS expectations, and ICH Q10 resource-management principles.*
