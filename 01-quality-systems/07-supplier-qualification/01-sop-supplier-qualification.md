# 01-SOP-SUP: Supplier Qualification and Oversight

> **Use this SOP to:** qualify, approve, monitor, change, and disqualify GMP suppliers and outsourced service providers.

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP ensures suppliers and contract acceptors are suitable, controlled, and monitored so purchased materials, services, and outsourced GMP activities do not compromise product quality, patient safety, data integrity, or regulatory commitments.

## 2. Scope

Applies to suppliers of:

- APIs, excipients, raw materials, packaging components, labels, printed materials
- contract manufacturing, packaging, testing, sterilization, storage, distribution, calibration, maintenance, cleaning, validation, and consulting services
- GMP computerized systems, SaaS providers, cloud/hosting services, data processing, and archival services
- critical equipment, instruments, utilities, and parts where supplier quality affects GMP state

## 3. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.22, 211.80, 211.84, 211.100, 211.160, 211.192.
- FDA Guidance for Industry: Quality Systems Approach to Pharmaceutical CGMP Regulations.
- EU GMP Chapter 1, Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- EU GMP Chapter 5, Production, where material controls apply.
- EU GMP Chapter 7, Outsourced Activities.
- EU GMP Annex 11, supplier and service provider controls for computerized systems.
- ICH Q9(R1), Quality Risk Management.
- ICH Q10, Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- ISPE GAMP 5 and GAMP IT/data integrity guides where computerized systems or data services are supplied.

## 4. Supplier Classification

| Class | Examples | Minimum oversight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | API, sterile component, contract manufacturer, contract lab for release testing, validated system SaaS provider, GDP storage/distribution provider. | Full qualification, quality agreement, audit or justified alternative, ongoing performance review, change notification, periodic requalification. |
| Major | Excipients, primary packaging, calibration service, maintenance service affecting qualified equipment, data archive provider. | Risk assessment, questionnaire/evidence review, agreement where needed, performance monitoring, requalification. |
| Minor | Low-risk indirect material or service with no direct GMP impact. | Approved supplier record and basic suitability check. |

Classification may be adjusted based on product criticality, supplier history, regulatory status, complexity, and availability of alternatives.

## 4.1 Supplier Scenario Rules

| Scenario | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| New critical supplier requested | Complete risk classification, qualification evidence, Quality approval, and quality agreement before routine GMP use. |
| Emergency supplier needed | Obtain Quality approval, define temporary controls and expiry, increase incoming checks where needed, and complete full qualification or stop use. |
| Supplier notifies site/process/specification change | Open change control, assess regulatory and validation impact, and block use until Quality disposition where impact is possible. |
| Supplier has critical audit finding | Place supplier on hold or conditional status, assess affected material/service, require CAPA, and define requalification criteria. |
| Supplier provides GMP data service or SaaS | Assess data integrity, access, audit trail, backup, retention, support access, incident notification, and exit controls. |

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier Quality / Quality Unit | Owns supplier qualification, approves supplier status, audits, agreements, disqualification, and requalification. |
| Procurement | Purchases only from approved suppliers and routes supplier changes or notices to Quality. |
| User Department / SME | Defines technical requirements and reviews supplier performance. |
| Regulatory Affairs | Assesses filing and market authorization impact. |
| System Owner / IT | Assesses computerized system, hosting, data, cybersecurity, and service controls. |
| Warehouse / QC | Performs receipt, sampling, testing, and discrepancy reporting. |

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Supplier request

Before first GMP use, document:

- supplier name, site, address, and service/material
- intended use and affected products/processes
- material/service specification
- criticality classification
- regulatory filing impact
- qualification plan and required evidence

### 6.2 Qualification assessment

Qualification may include:

- supplier questionnaire
- GMP certificate, manufacturing authorization, inspection history, or licenses
- quality manual or quality system summary
- audit report or on-site/remote audit
- technical capability review
- sample evaluation or trial batch
- method transfer or validation evidence
- CoA and test history
- data integrity controls
- change notification process
- business continuity and supply-chain controls
- cybersecurity, backup, restore, and data ownership review for computerized services

Qualification evidence must be reviewed and approved before routine GMP use unless an approved emergency or temporary supplier process is used.

### 6.3 Supplier audit

Audit need is risk-based.

Critical suppliers usually require an audit or a justified alternative, such as recent competent authority inspection, trusted third-party audit, or corporate audit report.

Audit scope should cover:

- quality system
- personnel training
- facilities/equipment
- materials and process controls
- laboratory controls
- deviation/CAPA/change control
- data integrity
- subcontracting
- complaint/recall support
- records and retention
- regulatory inspection history

### 6.4 Quality agreement

A written quality or technical agreement is required for critical outsourced GMP activities and should be considered for major suppliers.

Agreement topics:

- responsibilities of each party
- specifications and test methods
- batch/lot release and CoA expectations
- deviations, OOS/OOT, complaints, recalls, and investigations
- change notification and approval requirements
- subcontracting restrictions
- data integrity and record retention
- audit rights
- regulatory inspection notification
- transport/storage conditions
- confidentiality and knowledge transfer
- termination and record handover

### 6.5 Approval status

Supplier status may be:

- Approved
- Conditionally approved
- Temporary / emergency approved
- Quarantined / on hold
- Disqualified

Conditional or temporary approval must define limits, expiry, additional testing, or controls.

### 6.6 Ongoing monitoring

Monitor suppliers using:

- incoming inspection/testing results
- deviations, complaints, OOS/OOT, and rejects
- on-time delivery and supply reliability
- audit findings and CAPA
- change notifications
- regulatory warning letters/import alerts/inspection outcomes
- service-level performance
- data integrity or cybersecurity notices

### 6.7 Supplier changes

Supplier changes require change control when they affect:

- material grade, specification, site, process, test method, packaging, label, storage, transport, or supplier ownership
- outsourced GMP process or subcontractor
- computerized system version, hosting, data location, backup, support, or security controls
- regulatory filings or approved supply chain

### 6.8 Requalification

Requalification frequency is risk-based. Critical suppliers should be reviewed at defined intervals, usually every 1-3 years depending on risk and performance.

Requalification must consider:

- current approval status
- performance trends
- audit/CAPA status
- changes since last approval
- regulatory status
- complaints/deviations
- quality agreement currency

### 6.9 Disqualification

Disqualify or place on hold when:

- supplier cannot meet GMP or specification requirements
- critical audit findings remain unresolved
- serious data integrity concern exists
- unapproved change affects quality
- regulatory action creates unacceptable risk
- repeated poor performance persists

Assess affected batches, materials, records, and regulatory commitments before final disposition.

## 7. Records

Maintain:

- supplier request and classification
- qualification evidence
- audit reports and CAPA
- quality agreements
- approved supplier list status
- performance reviews
- change notifications
- requalification records
- disqualification records

## 8. Metrics

Trend:

- supplier deviations and rejects
- audit finding severity
- overdue supplier CAPA
- late change notifications
- conditional/temporary suppliers
- repeat supplier issues

## 9. Quick Checklist

- Is supplier criticality defined?
- Is qualification complete before GMP use?
- Is a quality agreement required and current?
- Are supplier changes controlled?
- Is supplier performance trended?
- Are outsourced data and records protected?

---

*This SOP aligns with EU GMP Chapter 7 outsourced activity expectations, FDA Quality Unit responsibilities for contracted product, ICH Q10 outsourced activity control, and risk-based supplier oversight principles.*
