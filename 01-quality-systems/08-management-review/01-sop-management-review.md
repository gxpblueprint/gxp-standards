# 01-SOP-MR: Management Review of the Pharmaceutical Quality System

> **Use this SOP to:** review PQS performance, make management decisions, assign resources, and track quality actions to closure.

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the process for periodic management review of the Pharmaceutical Quality System (PQS).

Management review ensures:

- senior management visibility of quality performance and risk
- timely escalation of significant issues
- adequate resources for GMP activities
- decisions and actions are recorded and followed
- continual improvement is driven by evidence

## 2. Scope

Applies to site, business unit, or corporate management reviews covering GMP operations, outsourced activities, suppliers, computerized systems, quality systems, product/process performance, and regulatory compliance.

## 3. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.22 and 211.25.
- FDA Guidance for Industry: Quality Systems Approach to Pharmaceutical CGMP Regulations.
- EU GMP Chapter 1, Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- ICH Q10, Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- ICH Q9(R1), Quality Risk Management.

## 4. Frequency

Management review must occur at a defined frequency, typically quarterly or at least annually depending on site size, product risk, and operational complexity.

Additional review is required after:

- critical deviation, recall, warning letter, import alert, significant inspection finding, or major data integrity issue
- serious supplier failure
- major product quality trend
- significant resource constraint
- repeated overdue CAPA or quality commitments

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Senior Management | Chairs or sponsors review, makes decisions, assigns resources, and approves priorities. |
| Quality Unit | Prepares agenda, quality data, risk summary, minutes, actions, and follow-up tracking. |
| Functional Leaders | Present performance, risks, constraints, and improvement plans for their areas. |
| Action Owners | Complete assigned actions and provide evidence. |
| Regulatory / QP, where applicable | Escalates regulatory, batch certification, market, or inspection issues. |

## 6. Standard Inputs

Review inputs should include:

- quality objectives and quality policy effectiveness
- deviations, OOS/OOT, investigations, and recurrence
- CAPA status and effectiveness
- change controls, temporary changes, and emergency changes
- complaints, recalls, returns, and market actions
- internal and external audit findings
- regulatory inspections, commitments, and correspondence
- supplier performance and outsourced activity oversight
- training compliance and qualification gaps
- data integrity metrics and audit trail/access review findings
- validation, qualification, CSV, and periodic review status
- process performance and product quality monitoring
- annual product quality review or product quality review outputs
- overdue quality records and aging
- quality risk register and high residual risks
- resource needs and constraints
- improvement opportunities

## 6.1 Meeting Sequence

1. Confirm previous action status.
2. Review critical and major quality events.
3. Review overdue CAPA, deviations, change controls, audit actions, and training.
4. Review high residual risks and resource constraints.
5. Decide actions, owners, due dates, and escalation path.
6. Record decisions in minutes.
7. Track actions until completion.
8. Escalate overdue or ineffective actions.

## 7. Procedure

### 7.1 Prepare

Quality prepares the agenda and data pack with:

- reporting period
- metrics and trends
- critical/major issues
- overdue actions
- risk escalations
- proposed decisions or resources needed

Data must be accurate, traceable, and not selectively reported.

### 7.2 Conduct review

The review must:

1. Confirm prior action status.
2. Review significant quality events and trends.
3. Assess whether processes remain in control.
4. Review high residual risks and resource needs.
5. Decide required actions, owners, and due dates.
6. Escalate issues requiring corporate, regulatory, QP, customer, or supplier involvement.
7. Identify continual improvement priorities.

### 7.3 Outputs

Management review outputs must include decisions and actions related to:

- improvement of the PQS and processes
- product quality or process performance improvement
- resource allocation
- risk reduction
- CAPA or change control initiation
- audit or supplier oversight adjustment
- training or staffing needs
- regulatory or customer communication

### 7.4 Follow-up

Quality tracks actions to completion. Overdue or ineffective actions are escalated to senior management and may require CAPA.

## 8. Records

Maintain:

- agenda and attendees
- data pack or references
- minutes
- decisions and rationale
- action log with owners/due dates
- action completion evidence
- escalations and follow-up reviews

## 9. Metrics

Track:

- management review completion on schedule
- overdue management review actions
- repeat escalations
- high residual risks
- resource actions completed
- quality objectives met/not met

## 10. Quick Checklist

- Did senior management attend or formally review outputs?
- Were significant risks and trends visible?
- Were decisions recorded with owners and dates?
- Were resource needs addressed?
- Were previous actions reviewed?
- Is follow-up tracked to completion?
