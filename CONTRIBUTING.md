# Public Use and Contribution Policy

GxP Blueprint is published for internal use under the GxP Blueprint Community Licence 1.0.

Every organization—including a large or multinational pharmaceutical company—may read, copy, use, and adapt the published material for its own internal operations with attribution. There is no company-size or revenue threshold.

Third-party resale, sublicensing, white-labelling, commercial redistribution, and substantial embedding in a paid product or service require prior written permission. Read the [licensing guide](https://www.gxpblueprint.com/licensing) and the [complete licence](https://www.gxpblueprint.com/licensing/gxp-blueprint-community-license-1.0.txt).

## External Contributions

This repository is not accepting external contributions at this time.

Do not submit:

1. Pull requests.
2. Patches.
3. Proposed SOP text.
4. Draft standards intended for inclusion in this repository.
5. Edits submitted with the expectation that they will be merged.

Pull requests are disabled for this repository.

## Feedback

General feedback may be reviewed, but it is not treated as a contribution request.

If you send feedback, GxP Blueprint may use the idea, concern, or correction without accepting contributed text, assigning authorship, or creating any obligation to include the feedback.

## Ownership

GxP Blueprint maintains the published standards and controls all changes to this repository.

Keeping the repository public does not mean the project is open for third-party authorship or community-maintained content.
