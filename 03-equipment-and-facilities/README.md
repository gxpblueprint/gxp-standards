# Equipment and Facilities

Reference procedures for qualifying equipment, controlling measurement performance, and managing maintenance and repair.

## Draft procedures for adaptation

These drafts cover general GMP manufacturing and quality-control equipment. They require equipment-specific review and approval before operational use.

- [Equipment Qualification](./01-sops/equipment-qualification.md) — planned evidence of suitability for intended use and continued control.
- [Calibration Management](./01-sops/calibration-management.md) — measurement limits, calibration records and failed or overdue equipment.
- [Preventive Maintenance and Repair](./01-sops/preventive-maintenance-and-repair.md) — planned work, breakdowns and verified return to service.

Facility and utility qualification, cleaning validation and specialist sterile-process controls need separate procedures.
